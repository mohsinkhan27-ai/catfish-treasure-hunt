Catfish Treasure Hunt

A cat-faced fish roams the vast 3D open ocean. Follow the treasure map and its signal to dig up 6 hidden chests — while dodging 3 apex predators across the depths!

  • Bubble Boost Speed Rings — Swim through floating glowing rings for high-speed turbo bursts with speed lines!
