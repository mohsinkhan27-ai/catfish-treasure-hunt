Catfish Treasure Hunt

A cat-faced fish roams the vast 3D open ocean. Follow the treasure map and its signal to dig up every hidden chest — but a cunning shark drains your life, and plants explosive fake treasures to lure you in.

  • WASD / On-screen Joystick — swim forward, backward & 360° steer
  • Space / Shift (or Up / Dive buttons) — ascend / dive in 3D
  • V (or View button) — cycle between 3rd Person, 1st Person & Bird's Eye views
  • M (or Map button) — open the 3D sonar navigation chart
  • Tablet / Mobile Ready — tap 📱 Touch in top bar anytime to toggle touch controls
  • Watch the Signal meter heat up as you near a treasure
  • Keep your Life bar up — the shark sucks life when close, but it refills when you escape
  • Beware explosive fake treasures the shark plants — a lit fuse costs you half your life!
