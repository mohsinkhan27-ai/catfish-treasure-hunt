Catfish Treasure Hunt

A cat-faced fish roams the vast 3D open ocean. Complete both tasks to win: recover all 6 hidden chests AND vanquish the Giant Sea Floor Octopus!

  • 🏆 Two Goals to Win — 1) Collect all 6 ancient chests. 2) Vanquish the Giant Octopus with your Bubble Blaster!
  • 🫧 Targeted Bubble Blaster — Found in a chest! Shoots straight laser-precise bubble torpedoes with F / Tap 🫧. Use the on-screen crosshair to snipe!
  • 🐙 Enraged Jump-Scare Charge — The octopus only attacks on the seafloor. Striking it with bubbles provokes a high-speed charging jump scare! Swim up to stay safe!
  • ❤️ Strict Lifeline Survival — Lifeline does NOT replenish! Every predator attack takes 10% max. Complete the game with your remaining life!
